How to play

Goal

Work out where everyone was at the moment of the murder. The murderer is the one person who was in the same room as the victim, with nobody else there.

Rules

  • Exactly one person per row and per column.
  • Nobody stands on blocking furniture (dark tiles: tables, shelves, plants, trees…). Beds, chairs, sofas, carpets, benches and cars can be occupied.
  • Furniture can cover several squares, but a person only takes one of them.
  • The victim is a person too: they count for rows, columns and every clue.

Controls

  • Tap a character card to select them. Press & hold a card to cross them off.
  • Tap a square: add or remove a small note.
  • Press & hold a square (or double-click with a mouse): place the character. Do it again to pick them up.
  • Right-click a square: mark it with an X.
  • Drag: paint notes across squares.

Tools

  • ✕: tap or drag to rule squares out. Placing someone fills their row and column automatically.
  • Eraser: tap a square to clear it; hold the button to clear the board.
  • Hint: the next logical step, with the squares it's about.
  • Submit: check your answer once everyone is placed.

Directly left, right, above or below, and in the same room.
with
In the same room. not with: in different rooms.
alone
Nobody else was in that room, not even the victim.
alone with
Only those two people were in the room.
nobody was in…
That room was completely empty.
corner
A square where a horizontal and a vertical wall of its room meet.
diagonal
On either diagonal line through the other person, across the whole grid.
row / column
The whole line across the grid, not just the room.
north / south / west / east of
Any square in a row above / below, or a column to the left / right, anywhere on the grid.
exactly one row north of
In the row directly above, any column.
top row / westernmost column
The edge lines of the whole grid.
first or second column / top two rows
Either of the two lines at that edge of the grid.
someone
Any other person, the victim included. “Two rows north of her, someone was beside a plant” means at least one person in the row two above hers stood beside a plant.
a Bedroom / the other Bedroom
When a house has two rooms of the same kind (North and South Bedroom), “a Bedroom” means either one, and “the other Bedroom” is the one this person is not in.
a woman / a man
At least one person of that gender, as shown by she/he on their card.
beside a wall, empty room on the other side
Next to a wall, and the room directly behind that wall had nobody in it.
… or …
At least one of the two is true.

1. Look for forced squares

If a row or column has only one open square, somebody is there, even if you don't know who yet.

2. Pairs lock lines

If two people are each confined to the same two columns, nobody else can use those columns. Same for rows.

3. Cross the intersections

If a person can only be in a few squares, any square that shares a row or column with all of them is ruled out for everyone else.

4. Try it and see

If putting someone on a square would leave somebody else with nowhere to go, that square is impossible.

5. Don't forget the victim

The victim's room holds exactly two people. That alone often places them.

Does “someone” include the victim?

Yes. Think of the scene just before the murder, while the victim was still alive.

Are the statements always true?

Yes. No tricks, no lies.

“Beside a chair”: could it be several chairs?

Yes, at least one. A clue would say “exactly one” if the number mattered. Someone beside a chair may also sit on a chair.

If he's lying in a bed, is he “beside a bed”?

Read it whichever way feels natural: every case is checked under both readings, so it never changes the answer.

Is there exactly one solution?

Yes, every case is checked by a solver. If you found another, re-read the clues carefully, and remember the victim must be alone with the murderer.

Do I ever have to guess?

No. Every case is solved step by step by a logic engine before it's published, and that's where the hints come from.

Do the portraits matter?

No, they're decoration. Only the clues count.
